What kitty is

A package that opens windows of the kitty terminal from Tuatara, reads their text into a buffer and types into them.

kitty is a terminal drawn by the graphics card, with ligatures and pictures. This package opens its windows from the editor. A window here belongs to a kitty that Tuatara starts itself, never your own kitty. The editor steers it over a socket of its own, and only over that socket.

It is a package, not part of the editor. kitty itself is by Kovid Goyal. The package drives it by its remote control.

1.1 How it fits together

Tuatara starts a kitty with a socket of its own in your runtime directory. Where you have none, the socket is in a directory of Tuatara's cache that only you may enter. Tuatara sends its commands to that socket with kitten, kitty's own command line tool, which has been part of kitty since 0.28.

Three commands do the work:

  • M-x kitty opens a window;
  • M-x kitty-text brings a window's text into a buffer;
  • M-x kitty-input types a line or the selection into a window.

1.2 What this manual covers

  • Chapter 2 is the use of the three commands.
  • Chapter 3 is about running Tuatara itself in a kitty of its own, and how to set the looks of the windows.
  • Chapter 4 says what keeps a program in a window from steering kitty, and lists the setting and the limits.
